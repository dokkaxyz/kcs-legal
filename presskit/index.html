The game

Slash demon bosses, dash through yokai and climb a tower that changes every time you play.

The tower changes shape every night. The shadow that killed your master waits at the top. One sword, one chance: die, and you fall back to the bottom, but you remember something.

  • Fast, fluid combat: parry, dash, execute, air combos.
  • 70+ cards and 22 paths: three cards of one class wake a path with its own living card; five bring its mastery.
  • Every climb is different: a procedural tower, 9 biomes, guardians and caves.
  • Nine ink-painted biomes that breathe: lightning over the storm sea, eyes in the silk caves, lanterns rising over the night city.
  • Painted in washi paper and indigo, with vermilion as the one accent.
  • The Daily Board: the same tower for everyone each day, and a prize for yesterday's best.
  • Duel a friend: send a short code, race up the same tower for 5 minutes, then fight on the temple roof.
  • Kiyomori waits at the summit: three endings shaped by how you climbed, then the Endless Tower.
  • A second weapon, the spear, and the Guardian Road: five guardians in a row against the clock.
  • The Soul Tree: 4 branches, 51 roots and 3 crowns of lasting growth.
  • Game Center: 3 leaderboards and 28 achievements.
  • Photo mode and Best Moments: the finest kills saved as GIFs.
  • Playable with one hand, with haptics and accessibility options.

About the developer

Akif Bay is an independent solo developer from Türkiye. He made Killer Climber Samurai alone, in Unity; the game and its App Store page are in 10 languages.
