Frequent questions

  • Cancel Samurai's Promise: iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • Refunds: purchases are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • My progress: it lives on your iPhone and in its iCloud or computer backup.
