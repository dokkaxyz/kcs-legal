Privacy Policy

Killer Climber Samurai · effective 2026-09-27

Killer Climber Samurai ("the game") is made by Akif Bay, an independent developer ("we"). This page explains what data is involved when you play and what you can choose.

1. What we collect ourselves

Nothing that identifies you. The game has no account and no servers of ours: your progress, settings and the record of your purchases are stored only on your iPhone (and in your device backup). The game does not send your usage data to us. If you delete the game, this data is deleted.

2. Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details; the App Store only confirms that a purchase was made so the game can deliver it.

3. Advertising

The game is free and shows ads through Unity LevelPlay, a service of Unity Technologies, and the ad networks it works with (such as Unity Ads). To show, measure and protect ads — for example to limit how often you see the same ad and to prevent fraud — these partners may collect: the advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), the vendor identifier (IDFV), your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device model, operating system, language, how you interact with ads and in-app purchase events. If you allow it, they also use this data to show ads that suit you better. Their use of data is described in Unity's privacy policy.

Rewarded ads are always your choice: you watch one only when you want its reward. "No Ads" and the "Samurai's Pledge" subscription remove interstitial ads.

4. Your choices

  • Tracking: iOS asks once whether the game may track you. You can change it any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Personalised ads: in the game, Settings → Gameplay → Personalised Ads. When it is off we tell the ad partners not to personalise ads and not to sell or share your data (withdrawn GDPR consent; CCPA "Do Not Sell or Share").
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Keeping and deleting data

We keep no personal data. Ad partners keep the data they collect under their own policies; you can reset your advertising identifier in iOS settings. To remove the game's data from your device, delete the game.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA) you may have the right to access, delete or object to the use of your data. For ad data, Unity's policy explains how to ask; we are glad to help with any question at doktoburu@proton.me.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the current version.

9. Contact

Akif Bay · doktoburu@proton.me

Seni tanımlayan hiçbir şey. Oyunda hesap yok, bize ait bir sunucu da yok. İlerlemen, ayarların ve satın alma kayıtların yalnızca iPhone'unda (ve cihaz yedeğinde) durur. Oyun, kullanım verini bize göndermez. Oyunu silersen bu veriler de silinir.

Ödemeleri Apple alır. Ödeme bilgilerini hiçbir zaman görmeyiz. App Store yalnızca bir satın almanın yapıldığını bildirir, oyun da ürünü teslim eder.
